From first call to certificate: how we work.

Security work should pay its way. Every stage ends with something your board can use: a decision, a plan, a budget, or proof your customers will accept.

The rule we follow

What the law requires first. What your customers ask for second. What wins you work third.

Most firms don't need every standard. We help you choose the ones that protect the business and open doors to bigger customers, and leave the rest.

Nine stages

You don't have to buy all nine stages. Most firms start with a Cyber Essentials gap assessment and decide from there.

  1. 1Free scoping call

    What happens
    A free 20-minute call. We ask what you sell, who buys it, where they are, and what's pushing you now: a customer questionnaire, a tender, your insurer, an incident.
    You get
    A short list: what the law requires of you, what your customers will ask for, and which standards would help you win work.
    Why it matters
    You stop paying for standards you don't need, and you know which one opens which door.

  2. 2Gap assessment

    What happens
    We test your controls once and report against every standard that matters to you, such as Cyber Essentials, Defence Cyber Certification, ISO 27001, SOC 2, PCI DSS and GDPR. The £695 / £995 half day covers Cyber Essentials; other standards take longer and are quoted after the scoping call.
    You get
    What's in place, what's partly there and what's missing, with the evidence for each.
    Why it matters
    One assessment instead of one per standard. It also answers most of the questions in your customers' security questionnaires.

  3. 3Risks in business terms

    What happens
    Each gap becomes a risk with an owner, rated by how likely it is and what it would cost the business: downtime, lost customers, fines, a failed tender.
    You get
    A risk register and a short list of top risks for the board.
    Why it matters
    The board decides on money and risk, not on technical jargon.

  4. 4Plan and budget

    What happens
    We put the fixes in order, with effort and cost. Quick wins first, and the tools you already pay for, such as Microsoft 365, before anything new.
    You get
    A 12-month plan and budget the board can approve.
    Why it matters
    Money goes where it cuts the most risk or unlocks the most sales.

  5. 5Policies that fit

    What happens
    Only the policies your standards need. We merge them with the ones you already have, in your own template, and flag any rule you don't yet meet, so the board never signs a policy the business already breaks.
    You get
    Short, approved policies your staff can read and follow.
    Why it matters
    Ready for an auditor, without a shelf of documents nobody reads.

  6. 6Fix and evidence

    What happens
    Your IT team or IT firm makes the changes. We check the work and keep the records auditors and customers ask for: assets, suppliers, risks, incidents and, if you use AI, your AI systems. If the gap assessment finds your computers and cloud services aren't locked down to a recognised standard, a hardening assessment checks every machine and gives your IT team a machine-by-machine fix list.
    You get
    An evidence pack, and a security pack for your sales team: a completed questionnaire, your policy list and test results.
    Why it matters
    A faster yes from larger customers, and less senior time spent on questionnaires.

  7. 7Certificate or report

    What happens
    An independent body certifies you (Cyber Essentials, ISO 27001) or a licensed auditor reports on you (SOC 2). We get you ready and stay with you through it.
    You get
    The certificate or report your customers and tenders ask for.
    Why it matters
    It turns security from a question in the buying process into a reason to choose you.

  8. 8Measure what matters

    What happens
    For firms that need security figures every month or week: we agree the measures and targets with your board, connect your data to the business intelligence dashboard you already use, and build the traffic lights and the board report. After handover, your team keeps the data up to date.
    You get
    A security dashboard and a one-page board report, with every measure set against a target your board has agreed.
    Why it matters
    The board sees whether the business is safe, not a count of blocked attacks.

  9. 9Keep it true

    What happens
    As your part-time security lead: re-test controls each quarter, report to the board, review policies each year, and help answer customer questionnaires.
    You get
    A security lead for your board, your customers and your insurer.
    Why it matters
    Security stays a sales asset, not a scramble once a year.

Questions

Do we have to do every stage?
No. Many firms start and stop with a Cyber Essentials gap assessment. You choose how far to go at the end of each stage.
Do you certify us?
No. Independent certification bodies and auditors do that. We get you ready to pass first time.
Do you make the technical changes?
We act as your independent security lead, not your IT support desk. We give your IT team or IT firm a prioritised fix list, brief them directly if needed, and check their work before you go for certification. Keeping oversight separate from the people doing the work is what keeps you protected.
We already have security policies. Do we start again?
No. We keep what works, update it, and add only what's missing, in your own template.
