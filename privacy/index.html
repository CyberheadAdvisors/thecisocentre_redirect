Privacy notice

This site uses no cookies and no tracking. This notice covers the personal data you share when you book a call, email us or ask for your self-check report.

Who we are

The CISO Centre is a trading name of Cyberhead Advisors Ltd, registered in England and Wales, company number 14593535. Registered office: 51 Larkdown, Wantage, Oxfordshire, OX12 8HF, United Kingdom. Contact: sean.muirhead@thecisocentre.co.uk.

What we collect and why

When you book a call or email us, we receive your name, email address, company and anything you tell us. We use it to reply, hold the call and, if you become a client, deliver and invoice the work. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering enquiries, and contract once you are a client.

Security self-check

If you ask for your self-check report, we collect your name, work email, company, number of staff, your answers and your score. We use them to send you the report, and so we can talk your results through with you if you book a call. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest. We only send marketing emails if you ticked the box, and you can opt out at any time. If you never open the report link, we delete your details after 30 days. Otherwise we keep them for 2 years from our last contact, then delete them. The data is stored by Microsoft Azure in the UK, and emails are sent through Microsoft 365. Ask us at any time to see or delete your data.

Assessments for clients

When we assess your business, we keep our scoping notes, your assessment answers and the reports we send you. They are stored encrypted in Microsoft Azure in the UK, and only Sean can open them. We use them to deliver and follow up the work. Notes from a scoping call that doesn't lead to work are deleted 2 years after our last contact; client records are kept for 6 years.

Documents you share

Before a call, you can choose to share your security policies or other documents with us. Sharing is optional. We collect the files you upload (PDF or Word, up to 20 files of 10 MB each), plus your name, company and work email. We use them to prepare for our call, so we can spend it on what matters to you. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest. The files are stored encrypted by Microsoft Azure in the UK, and only Sean Muirhead reads them. We don't use AI tools or other services to read them. We delete them 90 days after your last upload. If we start working together, we keep them with your client records and delete them when those are deleted. You can ask us to delete them at any time: reply to any of our emails or write to sean.muirhead@thecisocentre.co.uk. Please share only documents you're allowed to share.

Business contact details from public sources

We collect business contact details (name, job title, work email address and company) from public sources, such as company websites and Companies House. We use them to contact firms about The CISO Centre's services. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in telling businesses about a service that may help them. We keep these details for 24 months from our last contact with you, or until you object, whichever is sooner. To object, reply "no thanks" to any email from us, or email sean.muirhead@thecisocentre.co.uk. We will then stop contacting you and keep only your name and email address on a suppression list, so we do not contact you again.

Who we share it with

Our email, booking and self-check tools are provided by Microsoft. We do not sell your data or share it for marketing.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to work are deleted within 24 months. Client records are kept for 6 years for tax purposes.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to how we use it, by emailing us. If you are unhappy with our reply, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.
