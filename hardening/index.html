How locked down are your computers and cloud services, really?

Most firms set their devices up once and never check again. A hardening assessment reads the actual settings on every machine and cloud account, and gives your IT team a fix list in order.

Why it matters

Attackers look for the one machine that was missed.

Weak settings, such as disk encryption turned off, an old sharing setting left on or a cloud account without the right protections, are how small problems become breaches. Customers, insurers and certification bodies increasingly ask whether your systems follow a recognised standard. This assessment shows you where you stand, machine by machine.

How it works

  1. 1Scope and authority

    What happens
    A short call to agree which systems are in scope. You sign a written authority listing those systems, the dates, and exactly what data is read. Nothing runs until it is signed.

  2. 2Your IT team runs the check

    What happens
    Your IT team or IT firm sends our script to your machines through the device management tool you already use, and connects the cloud checks. It runs once, or daily for up to 14 days to catch machines that were offline. Then it stops and removes itself.

  3. 3Compare and report

    What happens
    We compare the results with public guidance from Microsoft, the NCSC, Apple and the US Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency, and widely used industry best-practice hardening settings.
    You get
    A list of every machine checked, any that were missed, and a fix list in priority order. Each item says why it matters and what to change.

  4. 4Your IT team fixes

    What happens
    Your IT team tests and makes the changes. We don't change your systems. When you're ready, we can run the check again to confirm the fixes held.

Safe by design

Does it change anything on our machines?
No. The script only reads settings. It doesn't change settings, open your documents or emails, or install anything that stays. Every item it reads is listed, with the reason, on the authority form you sign.
Can we see what it does?
Yes. The script is plain text your IT team can read from top to bottom before it runs.
Where do the results go?
Into a folder in your own Google Drive or SharePoint. When the work is done, you remove our access.
Does it run forever?
No. Each copy of the script has its end date built in. When it passes, the script removes itself and can't run again.
